Engineering Note

Install termination only at the two physical ends of the trunk, keep stubs short, and follow the cable manufacturer’s impedance and shielding recommendations.

FAQ

Where should 120 ohm termination resistors go?

Place them at the two ends of the main bus trunk, not at every device or branch.

What is the most common RS-485 wiring mistake?

Long T-shaped stubs, incorrect reference wiring, and termination placed in the middle of the bus are frequent causes of intermittent faults.
