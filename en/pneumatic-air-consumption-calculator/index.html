FAQ

Why is air consumption shown as normal liters per minute?

Normal liters per minute references air volume at a defined standard condition, allowing compressor capacity to be compared consistently.

Should the compressor equal the calculated flow exactly?

No. Include peak simultaneity, leakage, pressure losses, and reserve capacity.
