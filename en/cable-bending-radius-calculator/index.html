Engineering Note

Always use the cable manufacturer’s dynamic rating. Check travel speed, acceleration, torsion, unsupported length, separation, and carrier fill.

FAQ

Is static bend radius suitable for a drag chain?

No. Moving applications require the manufacturer’s dynamic bend radius and cycle-life rating.

How much free space should a cable carrier have?

Leave enough clearance for movement and heat, and follow the carrier and cable manufacturer’s fill and separation guidance.
