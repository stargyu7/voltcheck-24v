FAQ

Why does heat increase rapidly at higher C-rate?

Resistive heat follows I²R, so doubling current can increase the resistive component by roughly four times.

Does this replace cell-level thermal simulation?

No. It is a preliminary pack-level sizing tool; detailed CFD, abuse testing, and BMS validation remain necessary.
