Engineering Note

Check the servo drive manufacturer’s minimum resistance, peak power, continuous power, duty cycle, and thermal installation limits.

FAQ

Why is peak resistor power different from continuous power?

Deceleration energy may arrive in short pulses, while the resistor’s average thermal rating depends on repetition and duty cycle.

Can the drive capacitor absorb all regeneration energy?

Sometimes, but the result depends on bus voltage, capacitance, speed, inertia, and deceleration profile.
