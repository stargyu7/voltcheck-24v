ISO 281 Rating Life Equation

Basic rating life in operating hours is given by: L10h = (10^6 / (60 · n)) · (C / P)^p, where n is rotational speed (RPM), C is basic dynamic load rating (kN), P is dynamic equivalent load (kN), and exponent p = 3 for ball bearings (p = 10/3 for roller bearings).

Dynamic Equivalent Radial Load (P)

Bearings subjected to both radial load (Fr) and axial thrust load (Fa) calculate equivalent load as P = X · Fr + Y · Fa, factoring contact angle and radial/axial ratio (Fa / Fr > e).

Vibration Thresholds (ISO 10816)

Vibration velocity above 4.5 mm/s (RMS) on industrial pumps and electric motors signals significant bearing wear or unbalance. Above 11.2 mm/s represents extreme danger of catastrophic drive shaft seizure.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does 90% reliability mean in L10h?

L10h represents the number of operating hours that 90% of a group of identical bearings will achieve or exceed before the first evidence of material fatigue flaking appears.

Why do bearings fail prematurely even when load is within rating?

Over 80% of premature bearing failures stem from inadequate or contaminated lubrication, over-greasing leading to churn overheating, misalignment, and electrical shaft currents (VFD EDM fluting).
