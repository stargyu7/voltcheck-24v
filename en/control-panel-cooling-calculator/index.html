FAQ

When should I use a panel air conditioner?

Use an air conditioner when ambient temperature approaches or exceeds the target internal temperature, or when the enclosure must remain sealed.

Should I calculate heat from motor output power?

Use the electrical losses that become heat inside the enclosure, not the full mechanical output power.
