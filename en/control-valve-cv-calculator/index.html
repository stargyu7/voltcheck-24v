FAQ

Is a larger Cv always better?

No. An oversized valve can operate near its seat and lose controllability. Select a practical operating range around the normal flow point.

What is the difference between Cv and Kv?

Cv is commonly expressed in US flow units while Kv uses metric flow conventions; the calculator provides the selected basis.
